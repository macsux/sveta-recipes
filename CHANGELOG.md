# What's new

## 1.4.0 — 2026-10-07

- **Point at part of the app** for the assistant: press the pin button (above the send button), then click anything in
  the window. The assistant gets a picture of it with what you clicked outlined, so you can say "this box" or "this
  button". Point at several things before sending if you like.
- **Development mode** (the "Mode" button at the top, next to Assistant): the assistant can change the app itself. Ask
  for a new button, a different layout or a new report; it makes the change, tests it, and a bar at the top offers
  **Apply changes**, which restarts the app with it. The first switch sets everything up by itself (a few minutes,
  nothing to click). Switch back to Release at any time. Where the app's code is kept can be changed under Tools &
  settings → Assistant.
- The mode button and the About page show exactly which version you're running.
- New versions are noticed while the app is open (it looks every half hour), not only when you start it.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-07

- The assistant no longer adds a recipe unless you ask it to. Paste a recipe or a link and it tells you whether you
  already have it or something close, then asks if you want it added.
- New **Assistant** page in Tools & settings: read and change the instructions the assistant follows. "Reset to
  default" brings back the original.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-07

- The assistant can now search the web and open web pages. Give it a link to a recipe and it reads the page, checks
  whether you already have it, and adds it.
- When an update has downloaded, a bar at the top offers to restart into the new version (or it installs when you
  close the app, as before).
- New **About** page in Tools & settings: the version you have and what changed in each release.
- Assistant panel: the switch that shows or hides its steps is now the wrench button at the top, next to "+".

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-07

- **Assistant** panel on the right (Assistant button, top right). Ask about your recipes, costs, ingredients,
  customers and invoices in plain words.
- Paste a recipe from a book or website: the assistant checks whether you already have it or something close, and
  adds it if not, tagged "Imported". New ingredients are listed so you can price them.
- It makes a backup before it changes anything.
- Conversations are saved and carry on where you left off; "+" starts a new one.
- Every step it takes (looking in your data, costing, comparing recipes) can be opened to see exactly what it did.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-05

- The installer sets up Microsoft .NET by itself when the PC doesn't have it, so updates are much smaller.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-05

- First version: replaces the Access "Sveta Recipe Database", with all your recipes, ingredients, prices, customers,
  suppliers and invoices brought over.
- Costs are always up to date: a price change reaches every recipe that uses the ingredient, including through
  sub-recipes.
- Recipes, ingredients, shopping lists, invoices, customers & suppliers, unit conversions, labels and printing.
- Your data lives in its own folder, separate from the program, and is backed up every day.
- Updates install themselves.
